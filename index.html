Putt Lab

Leave it on and roll putts. Each time the ball leaves your spot it reads the stroke, calls out tempo, start line and path, and replays it with the lines drawn on.

  1. Phone on the tripod above your putter, camera down, set out past the far side of your line.
  2. Drop a coin on your line 1–2 ft ahead of the ball, in the picture.
  3. Start the camera and tap the ball, then the coin. Then just putt.
