Putt Lab

Your putting sim. Leave it on, roll putts, and it reads every stroke the moment the ball leaves your spot: tempo, start line, putter path and backstroke, called out loud.

  1. Phone on the tripod arm above your putter, camera down, out past the far side of your line. Hold it sideways.
  2. Drop a coin on your line 1–2 ft ahead of the ball, inside the picture.
  3. Start the camera, tap your ball, then the coin. After that it arms itself every time a ball sits still on the spot.
